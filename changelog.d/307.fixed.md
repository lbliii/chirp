`app.check()` no longer emits a false-positive `oob_target` warning for chirp-ui's own OOB helper macros (e.g. `context_rail_oob` targeting `chirpui-context-rail`). The OOB-target check now skips vendored `chirpui/` templates, symmetric with id-collection, since chirp-ui owns its internal OOB-target consistency.
